## How to do an oil change
You will need:
- A quart of oil
- A torque wrench
- A breaker bar
- A car jack
- At least 2 jackstands
- A new oil filter
- A drain pan (A bucket will work if you don't have one)
- A roll of clean rags
- Disposable gloves 

Instructions:
- 1: Jack up the car enough that you can comfortably crawl under it and have enough space to move your hands around
- 2: Lower it onto the jackstands. This is important as you don't want to rely on the jack to keep you from getting crushed. Make sure you still have enough room.
- 3: Now make sure you are wearing your gloves as this is the messy part. Place the drain pan underneath the drain plug. Then use your breaker bar to undo the drain plug and let the oil fully drain. 
- 4: Rub some fresh oil on your new filter's rubber gasket. Then unscrew the old filter and replace it with the new one. You should be able to use your hands for this part but it will be messy so keep your gloves on. 
- 5: Screw in the drain plug and tighten it with the torque wrench according to the manufacturers specifications
- 6: Get out from underneath the car and remove all your equipment. Then, jack up the car until it's off the jackstands and remove the stands then lower the car onto the ground. 
- 7: Finally pour in the new oil by removing the fill plug and pouring in the amount specified by the manufacturer.
